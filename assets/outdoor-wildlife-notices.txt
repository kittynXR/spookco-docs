SpookCo modifications: short excerpts, mono conversion, resampling, gentle
endpoint fades, distance filtering, quiet gain calibration, and two restrained
original early reflections. No pitch shifting or synthetic animal replacement
was used. The two owl clips excerpt distinct hoots from one recording. Dog
clips contain isolated single barks from a source distinct from Hound gameplay
vocals. No fox scream, repeated bark chorus, or Hound sample is used here.

SpectralAirSoft is original seeded SpookCo synthesis and processing: four
lower, slower spectral grains in an exact 20-second loop. It contains no
third-party recordings, samples, or room impulse responses. Existing rain,
wind, footstep and gameplay vocal sources are preserved.

Original bytes, creator-page snapshots, CC0 evidence, hashes, and editable
recipes are retained locally in AudioWork/OutdoorAmbienceV2. The two MP3
preview sources remain explicitly identified throughout that provenance.
