Three stops. One questionable employer.

SpookCo: Last Stop is a cooperative horror world built around short expeditions and a dependable refuge. Play alone or bring a crew of up to 16. Finish the work, scavenge what you can, and decide when to make a run for the van.

Clock in

Ready up at dispatch, choose your equipment, and read the contract. A new stop brings a new job and a different hunter.

Do the work

Recover cargo and repair equipment. Field supplies are shared: whoever collects a pickup gets it. Running is faster, but it carries farther through the dark.

Make it back

The van’s covered campfire refuge stays safe. Complete the quota to open extraction early, or scramble aboard during emergency extraction. Departure has a deadline.

A small world with a long night ahead

Two prototype locations are playable: Camp Deadwood and Last Chance Service Station. Six killers have different hunting patterns, with sight, movement hearing, and searches that follow recent evidence.

This is a playable development build, with placeholder environments, characters, and sound. The basic loop works; the next focus is game and environment design. Balance, visuals, and features will change. Spectator sensing radar and AI diagnostics are test tools and may be changed or removed before release.
