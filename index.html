One contract. Get the crew home.

SpookCo: Last Stop is a cooperative horror world built around short expeditions and a dependable refuge. Play alone or bring a crew of up to 16. Finish the work, scavenge what you can, and decide when to make a run for the van.

Clock in

Ready up at dispatch, choose your equipment, and read the contract. Stay ready for future rounds; the host starts each departure.

Do the work

Recover cargo and repair equipment. Field supplies are shared: whoever collects a pickup gets it. Running is faster, but it carries farther through the dark.

Make it back

The van’s covered campfire refuge stays safe. Complete the quota to open extraction early, or scramble aboard during emergency extraction. Departure has a deadline.

A small world with a long night ahead

Contracts use the authored destinations: Camp Deadwood, Last Chance Service Station, Calder laboratory, and Briarwood High School. Ten spooks use different senses and attack patterns. Some follow movement noise or old trails; others disguise themselves or warn before a lethal charge, catch, or gunshot. Quiet movement, cover, doors and knowing when to move matter.

Required work earns rewards and opens extraction. Once the contract is complete, optional bonus salvage offers more XP and cash within the existing departure deadline. Repaired generators recharge nearby active gear faster. Nineteen perks, eleven consumables, five reusable tools and persistent job/spook mastery provide choices across shifts; standing around to survive does not earn a round bonus.

This is a playable build still early in development, and it may change significantly after the game jam. Gameplay, progression, maps, and art are all subject to change. The local expansion includes dressed maps, darker dispatch lighting, distance fog and a glowing lounge CRT. Balance, device performance and multiplayer edge cases still need playtesting. Spectator sensing radar and AI diagnostics remain development tools and may change before release.
