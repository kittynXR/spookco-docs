Creator & Creative Director

Game design, world direction, and production of SpookCo: Last Stop — a cooperative horror world for VRChat.

Platform & development tools

  • Platform: VRChat SDK and UdonSharp.
  • Development tools: Blender, Clip Studio Paint, Affinity, and Codex.
  • Lighting: VRC Light Volumes by RED_SIM (MIT) carries the crew's flashlight beams through the world's surface shaders.
  • In-world fonts: Noto Sans JP from the Noto CJK project supports English and Japanese UI under the SIL Open Font License; its license ships with the world. Liberation Sans, supplied through TextMesh Pro, is also credited in the world's Credits page with its complete font license.

The website uses system fonts. Its night-shift illustration is original SVG artwork, not a gameplay screenshot.

Shader acknowledgments

  • VRChat and Xiexe: Toon shading uses VRChat's Mobile/Toon Lit, based in part on XSToon by Xiexe, with additional modifications for SpookCo.
  • Unity Technologies: Additional shader components are based on Unity's built-in shaders.
  • RED_SIM: The Depot Surface and Fogged Toon Lit shaders include the VRC Light Volumes shader library (MIT), which draws the flashlight beams.
  • David Hoskins and David A. Roberts: The Depot Surface grain helper uses Hash without Sine, with an HLSL adaptation based on Roberts's MIT-licensed publication.

Read the complete shader copyright and license notices.

Inspiration

SpookCo draws on short-round horror and scavenging games. No characters, models, audio, or code from Terrors of Nowhere or SlashCo are included.
