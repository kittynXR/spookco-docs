Progression & saving

The save system is implemented, but reconnect and multi-instance edge cases still need expanded uploaded testing. Do not treat a development build as a permanent economy guarantee.

What stays with you

Resource Purpose Intended lifetime
XP Levels and perk-point capacity Permanent across visits
Commendations Lifetime equipment milestones; never spent Permanent across visits
Career mastery Job and spook ranks; cosmetic crew titles Permanent across visits
Cash Buy consumables The live instance session
Consumable pouch Purchased and scavenged supplies The live instance session
Build and controls Perk/equipment choices and device preferences Saved across visits

Rejoining the same live session is designed to restore cash, pouch contents, and relevant equipment state. A → B → A uses separate records while A remains live. Up to 128 recent sessions are retained. When everyone leaves, that session ends; a later fresh session begins with fresh cash and supplies.

Wait for restoration before shopping or collecting. Accepted changes are saved during play, not just on departure. A sudden crash can lose the most recent cloud write. Capture does not remove banked XP or commendations.

Earn your contract bonus

Moving around or waiting in the van does not count as work. Contribute by delivering cargo, bringing fuel to a generator, completing or meaningfully helping a repair, rescuing a teammate, or using gear to interrupt an active chase or warning.

Result XP Cash Commendations
A contract your crew completes and you contributed to 25 10 1
You also extract alive from that completed contract 15 5 1 more
Failed contract, even if you reach the van No round bonus No round bonus 0
You did not contribute No round bonus No round bonus 0

Round XP is provisional until extraction. Failing to extract keeps only 10% of your earned round XP. Every dispatched player who fails to extract also reduces everyone's round XP by 10%, capped at 50%. Apply the personal reduction first, then the crew reduction, and round the final total down. A player who misses extraction with one crew member missing keeps 9%; with five or more missing, they keep 5%. A player who extracts with two missing keeps 80%.

Only the current round's XP is reduced. Previously banked XP, cash and commendations are untouched. The dispatched roster includes disconnected players; spectators and later arrivals do not add penalties. A failed contract provides no contract or extraction bonus. If you contributed and the crew completes the contract after you are eliminated, its XP bonus is included in the reduced total. Cancelling a live contract settles it as a failed extraction.

Cargo and repair finishers earn 10 / 12 / 20 / 30 XP for an initial crew of 1–2 / 3–4 / 5–8 / 9+ respectively. Cargo pays $8, repair pays $12, and fully fueling a generator pays $4 with the same crew-scaled XP. A player who supplies the first can to a two-can generator receives helper credit when a teammate supplies the second. Scrapper increases objective cash by 20%.

Optional bonus salvage unlocks only after the required contract is complete. Its finisher earns 20 / 24 / 40 / 60 XP for the same initial-crew brackets and $24 per case. Qualified handoff helpers receive half; Scrapper applies normally. Cash is awarded on delivery; the XP remains subject to extraction penalties. There are only one to three bonus cases per stop, and each pays once per trip, including restarts.

A cargo helper must carry the case at least three metres before handing it off. A repair helper must supply at least 10% of the completed repair. Qualified helpers receive half the objective XP and cash when someone finishes the task. Picking up and dropping a case or tapping a generator is not enough. Restarting a stop does not pay the same objective again.

Rescue credit is bounded to 10 XP / $3 per rescuer per stop. Gear that successfully interrupts an active threat can grant 8 XP / $3 once per stop; repeatedly distracting an idle spook grants nothing.

Each round ends after one contract. There is no additional three-stop shift payout. Contribute to a completed contract and extract alive to earn 40 base XP, $15 and 2 commendations, on top of your completed work rewards. The XP total still receives any crew extraction penalty.

The result screen shows your outcome, total cash, XP and commendations, then the return-to-dispatch countdown. Each reward line explains its use. The menu keeps the work, contract and extraction breakdown and next equipment unlock. Commendations is the current name for survival points; existing balances retain their value and are never spent.

Career mastery and titles

Open Perks, page past Specialist perks and More perks, then choose Career mastery. Two career pages show your counts, next rank and title choices. Select an earned title to display it beside your name in the crew roster, or choose No title to hide it. Titles automatically show your current rank, I, II or III. They are cosmetic and give no combat advantage.

Track What counts Rank I / II / III
Hauler Paid cargo deliveries, including bonus salvage and qualified handoff assists 5 / 25 / 100
Engineer Paid generator fuel and completed repairs, including qualified repair assists 5 / 25 / 100
Crew Support Paid rescues and useful gear support; each category counts once per stop 5 / 25 / 100
Each of the ten spooks Contribute to a completed contract and extract alive from that spook 3 / 10 / 25

Finishing work and meaningfully helping earn equal mastery credit, even though helper XP and cash are smaller. Merely surviving, failing the contract or missing extraction gives no spook mastery. Restarting the same stop cannot repeat its paid mastery. Job progress already earned survives a failed stop.

Older records that remember surviving a spook begin with one proven escape for that spook. They cannot reconstruct old job counts. Mastery and title selection save with your permanent profile.

Levels and perk points

Start at level 1 with 2 points. Gain a point at levels 2, 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18, 21, 24, 28, 32, 36 and 40, reaching 15 points at level 40. The early gains let you afford a four-point perk at level 3. Choose any combination of unique perks within your budget and respec freely during briefings.

The three-page perk menu shows cost, required level and equipped state. The original ten perks have no extra level requirement. Saved affordable builds keep their selections.

Combined gear and perk movement bonuses are capped at 20%; Sprint soda's 30% adds on top, and Braced separately improves sideways movement by 15%. Perks support different roles; required tasks remain possible with the starter loadout.

XP comes from completed work, bounded rescue/support credit, contributed contract and extraction results. Idle waiting, movement alone, and supply collection do not generate XP. The early level curve stays unchanged through level 10. Each subsequent level costs the base amount (150 + 35 × (current level − 1)) multiplied by the current band:

Current level XP cost multiplier

Level 10 requires 2,610 banked XP, level 15 7,960, level 20 25,710, and level 40 456,310. Perk-point milestones stay spaced across those levels; the budget still caps at 15. Later permanent equipment requires both a level and commendation milestone. Actual days or hours to reach these milestones depend on contributions and extraction success and need live balance data.
