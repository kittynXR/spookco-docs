Progression & saving

The save system is implemented, but reconnect and multi-instance edge cases still need expanded uploaded testing. Do not treat a development build as a permanent economy guarantee.

What stays with you

Resource Purpose Intended lifetime
XP Levels and perk-point capacity Permanent across visits
Survival points Lifetime equipment milestones; never spent Permanent across visits
Cash Buy consumables The live instance session
Consumable pouch Purchased and scavenged supplies The live instance session
Build and controls Perk/equipment choices and device preferences Saved across visits

Rejoining the same live session is designed to restore cash, pouch contents, and relevant equipment state. A → B → A uses separate records while A remains live. Up to 128 recent sessions are retained. When everyone leaves, that session ends; a later fresh session begins with fresh cash and supplies.

Wait for restoration before shopping or collecting. Accepted changes are saved during play, not just on departure. A sudden crash can lose the most recent cloud write. Capture does not remove banked XP or survival points.

Levels and perk points

Start at level 1 with 2 points. Gain one point every three levels, reaching 15 points at level 40. Choose any combination of unique perks within your budget and respec freely during briefings.

Perk Cost Current effect
Spotter 1 Objective markers visible 50% farther away
Courier 2 Removes the cargo movement penalty
Mechanic 2 Repairs 25% faster
Field Medic 2 Rescue interaction takes two seconds
Scrapper 2 20% more objective cash
Soft Soles 3 Movement hearing range reduced by 25%
Adrenaline 3 Extraction movement bonus
Quickdraw 3 Active equipment cooldowns reduced by 20%
Hardhat 4 One extra maximum health
Second Wind 4 Six-second field recovery on first down each stop

Combined positive movement bonuses are capped at 20%. Perks support different roles; required tasks remain possible with the starter loadout.

XP comes from objectives, bounded rescue participation, participated contract/extraction results, and trip results. Idle waiting and supply collection do not generate XP. The level curve totals 31,785 XP to level 40. A 20+ hour route to full capacity is a design target, not a measured result yet.
