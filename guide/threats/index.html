Know the threats

One killer is active at a time. They keep pace with a running employee, and they get faster the bigger the crew — at eight or more they outrun you outright. Only Roadkill is slightly slower, because its charge does the work. None of these descriptions guarantees an easy escape.

Killer What it does What to try
Groundskeeper Sight pursuit, remembered routes, and local searches Break sight, change route, and quietly leave its search area.
Rattletrap Blind; investigates movement and task noise Walk when possible, time repairs, and use a decoy.
Roadkill Telegraphs a fast, committed charge Dodge sideways and put solid cover between you and its line.
Hitchhiker Uses concealed authored ambush positions Watch roadside silhouettes and change approach.
Usher Warns before committing from its vision cone Get out of the cone or behind cover before the lock completes.
Switchboard Pursues and marks work-site/route hazards Leave the marked area and switch sites.

Your footsteps matter

All killers hear movement in every direction, including behind cover. Running can be heard farther away than walking. Quiet, stationary players do not continuously give away a new movement position. Soft Soles reduces movement hearing ranges by 25%.

This senses in-game movement and certain task events. It does not listen to microphone audio or voice chat.

One corner is not a guaranteed escape

Hunters can continue toward the last observed route, investigate sound, scan while moving, and check nearby paths. Current search budgets vary from 12 to 22 seconds and three to five search locations, depending on the killer. Search can finish when its point budget is exhausted.

Break sight, change route, reduce noise, and use the protected refuge when needed. Sound gives the AI an investigation point; it is not treated as direct vision through a wall.

Running in a straight line is no longer an escape. Hunters lead their turns instead of trailing you, and inside about three metres they lunge. A landed hit costs them a moment's recovery, which is the gap you use to reach cover, a door or the van. Carrying a case slows you below their pace: drop it if you are being run down.

Doors, and a field that changes every stop

Worksite front doors can be opened and closed. Shut one behind you and the hunter has to deal with it: an unhurried one opens it, but a hunter that is chasing you breaks it off its hinge, and a broken door cannot be closed again. A door buys you distance once, so spend it well. Nobody can close a door on a person standing in the doorway, and the rear opening of every worksite stays clear so you are never sealed in.

The field itself is rolled again at the start of every stop: hay bales, broken-down vehicles, fallen trees, crates, oil drums and more appear in different places, alongside the usual random supplies. Learn the landmarks, not the route.

Spectating the current build

Choose Watch / Withdraw to remain at dispatch. The Live Field Map shows crew and killer tracking. In the current test build, a gold outline depicts vision and blue rings depict walking/running hearing; Rattletrap has hearing only. Tracking is for lobby spectators, not active or downed crew.

Development feature: visible sensing radar and AI diagnostics may change or disappear from the final game. The map is a simplified horizontal view, not a guarantee of detection at every height or through every shape of cover.
