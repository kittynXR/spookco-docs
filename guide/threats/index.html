Know the threats

One killer is active at a time. Melee hunters keep pace with a running employee and gain speed with larger crews. Warden and Deadeye move more slowly, but threaten firing lanes at a distance. Read the warning and use each killer's recovery window.

Killer What it does What to try
Groundskeeper Sight pursuit, remembered routes, and local searches Break sight, change route, and quietly leave its search area.
Rattletrap Blind; investigates movement and task noise Walk when possible, time repairs, and use a decoy.
Roadkill Telegraphs a lethal, committed charge Dodge sideways and put solid cover between you and its line.
Hitchhiker Ambushes from concealed positions; warned catches are lethal Watch roadside silhouettes and change approach.
Usher Warns before a lethal chase from its vision cone Get out of the cone or behind cover before the lock completes.
Switchboard Pursues and marks work-site/route hazards Leave the marked area and switch sites.
Hound Follows aging movement trails as well as sight Creep, double back, and leave the old trail behind. Trailbreaker helps.
Mimic Disguises itself as false cargo, then warns before revealing Watch for eyes, twitching and creaks. Back away; Inspector can identify it.
Warden Warns, commits to a shotgun fan, fires, then reloads Leave the fan or reach solid cover. Move while it reloads.
Deadeye Confirms sight, warns with a rifle line, commits and fires Break sight before the red lock or sidestep afterward. It changes position between shots.

Your footsteps matter

Creeping is quiet for ordinary killers. Footsteps grow louder gradually as you move faster, and solid cover greatly reduces their reach. Rattletrap is the hearing specialist: it can detect slower movement and hear more through cover. Running is much riskier than walking. Soft Soles reduces movement hearing ranges by 25%.

A brief head-only or body-only peek builds suspicion before a sighted killer confirms you. Repeated peeks accumulate suspicion, and showing both your head and torso confirms you immediately. Once a killer is tracking you, a currently visible head or torso is enough to maintain a recent chase. Full cover still breaks sight, and cover does not erase a route the killer already saw.

This senses in-game movement and certain task events. It does not listen to microphone audio or voice chat.

Hiding in shrubs

Dense shrubs conceal you from ordinary monster vision and reduce your walking, running and sideways movement speed by 50% while your body is inside. Leaving restores your normal speed. The slowdown stacks with carried cargo, perks and temporary effects.

Break sight before entering. A monster actively chasing or aiming at you can keep tracking you if it watches you enter. If you break its view, it can still search the last place it saw you, but cannot simply reacquire you through the shrub. Standing on top of foliage does not count as hiding.

Bushes do not suppress hearing, erase Hound trails, stop committed bullets or charges, or make you invulnerable. Rattletrap remains a hearing specialist. Quiet movement and a route change still matter.

Cars, trailers, equipment and wooden fences provide solid cover. Chain-link fencing blocks passage while allowing sight through it; plan around its openings.

One corner is not a guaranteed escape

Hunters can continue toward the last observed route, investigate sound, scan while moving, and check nearby paths. They pursue fresh evidence promptly, then search more carefully. Search budgets vary by killer: running out of nearby routes does not instantly erase their memory, and a temporarily blocked path can lead to a local search. Warden and Deadeye also search beyond simply reaching your last known position. Quietly escaping still works; without fresh evidence every search ends. Escape Artist shortens visual searches; Trailbreaker shortens sound searches and makes Hound trails fade sooner.

Break sight, change route, reduce noise, and use the protected refuge when needed. Sound gives the AI an investigation point; it is not treated as direct vision through a wall.

Running in a straight line is unreliable against melee hunters. They lead their turns instead of trailing you, and inside about three metres they can lunge. A landed hit costs them a moment's recovery, which is the gap you use to reach cover, a door or the van. Carrying a case slows you below their pace: drop it if you are being run down.

Lethal attacks

Roadkill's charge, a warned catch from Hitchhiker or Usher, and a hit from Warden or Deadeye eliminate you for the rest of the round. Hardhat and Second Wind cannot save you from these attacks. Spawn and rescue immunity still protect you. Hitchhiker and Usher must warn before committing; breaking sight cancels their lethal chase, and a missed commitment gives you a recovery window. Solid cover stops contact through walls.

You can spectate from dispatch, and you join the next host-started round if you remain ready. If the whole crew is eliminated, the stop ends promptly. Eliminated players do not reduce the required task quota, and reconnecting or toggling readiness does not undo elimination.

Reading a gun warning

Amber aim follows you during the warning. Red aim is committed to a fixed line or fan: a late sidestep can avoid it. Solid cover blocks the actual shot, including after commitment. Smoke can break acquisition or an uncommitted aim, but it cannot stop a bullet already aimed at your position.

Decoys redirect an uncommitted gun killer; they cannot cancel its warning once aiming starts. A successful flash or trip alarm interrupts it and makes it confirm you again. Warden must reload after its shotgun blast. Deadeye reloads and looks for a new firing position. Neither deals melee contact damage.

False cargo and old trails

Mimic cargo is separate from real objectives. It never takes away delivered work. Its breathing, eyes and twitch are clues before the reveal; Inspector identifies a nearby visible disguise. Smoke can conceal your approach, and the reveal warning gives you time to back away.

The Hound follows recorded movement only after a delay. Quiet creeping leaves no new trail; old samples expire instead of tracking your hidden position. It can still see you normally, so use cover as you leave the trail.

Doors, and a field that changes every stop

Worksite front doors can be opened and closed. Shut one behind you and the hunter has to deal with it: an unhurried one opens it, but a hunter that is chasing you breaks it off its hinge, and a broken door cannot be closed again. A door buys you distance once, so spend it well. Nobody can close a door on a person standing in the doorway, and the rear opening of every worksite stays clear so you are never sealed in.

The field itself is rolled again at the start of every stop: hay bales, broken-down vehicles, fallen trees, crates, oil drums and more appear in different places, alongside the usual random supplies. Learn the landmarks, not the route.

Spectating the current build

Choose Watch / Withdraw to remain at dispatch. The Live Field Map shows crew and killer tracking. In the current test build, a gold outline depicts vision and blue rings depict walking/running hearing; Rattletrap has hearing only. Tracking is for lobby spectators, not active or downed crew.

Development feature: visible sensing radar and AI diagnostics may change or disappear from the final game. The map is a simplified horizontal view, not a guarantee of detection at every height or through every shape of cover.
