About SpookCo

SpookCo is a night maintenance company. This page covers who you work for, what the work is and what else is on site. The rest of the field guide covers getting home.

The work

SpookCo holds the maintenance contracts nobody else will take. When a site's caretaker stops answering invoices, the contract goes to auction and SpookCo bids. The contracts were inexpensive. The reason is on site.

A contract pays while the equipment runs and company property is accounted for. That is the whole business. So every night a crew drives out, starts the generators (maintenance performed), brings the cargo back (property accounted for) and leaves before departure.

Company property means the cargo cases, the generators, the lantern and the van. All contracts are night contracts. There is no day shift.

Dispatch and the auction

Dispatch is Precast Unit 03, at the end of Canal Street. It is the only SpookCo building.

It was bought at auction with its contents: one van, one trailer, one red hurricane lantern and a box of letterhead already printed SPOOKCO. The buyer kept the name. Reprinting costs money.

The office has a desk, a lamp and a phone. Management signs every contract. Management remains at dispatch. Nobody has seen Management at dispatch. Questions may be left in writing.

Spooks

Every site had staff. Every staff member had a job. The job did not end.

Whatever is still on a site is called a spook. Spooks keep to their old duties: the paths are swept, the calls are connected, the property is guarded. Being on duty is what makes them dangerous. They are not angry. They are thorough.

Spooks keep out of the lantern's light and leave the van alone. The only explanation on file is that the lantern is company property, and spooks respect company property. The lantern is the only company property that has never gone missing. After an escape, spooks return to the field. They have rounds to make.

For warnings, senses and escape routes, see Know the spooks.

Home sites

Three spooks have a home site on file:

  • The Groundskeeper belongs to Camp Deadwood.
  • Roadkill belongs to Last Chance Service Station.
  • The Switchboard belongs to Calder Nuclear Laboratory.

Briarwood's records are incomplete.

All three still turn up everywhere. SpookCo buys its contracts in bundles, and staff on one contract count as staff on every site in the bundle. Management considers this efficient.

Most spooks have no home site at all. They go wherever the work is, and at night the work is wherever SpookCo is.

Company vocabulary

Word Meaning
spook Whatever is on a site.
crew The employees on a contract, also called the night crew. This includes you.
dispatch Precast Unit 03, at the end of Canal Street. The only SpookCo building, and the voice on paper.
Management The signature on every contract. Remains at dispatch.
retained The company word for an employee who missed departure. Retained employees are not listed as absent.
departure The van leaving the site. It leaves on schedule.
