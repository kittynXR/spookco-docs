Dispatch FAQ

Who makes SpookCo?

SpookCo: Last Stop is a catnet systems production, created by kittyn, the Creator & Creative Director responsible for game design, world direction, and production. See the credits for the studio identity, development tools, and platform acknowledgments.

Is this the finished game?

No. SpookCo is still early in development and may change significantly after the game jam. The jam build is a working version, and its gameplay, progression, maps, and art are not final. Expect placeholder art and sound, changing balance, and unfinished edge cases. Spectator radar and AI diagnostics are development tools that may be removed. PC, Android/Quest, and iOS bundles have been built; broad device and multiplayer acceptance is still in progress.

How many people can play?

The implemented participant limit is 16, and solo play is supported. Handling the creator/instance-creator overflow case of 18 people in an instance is accepted work but is not implemented yet. Do not rely on overflow admission for a playtest.

Can I pass control of the game to someone else?

Yes. Open Host, select a player, and offer Pass Host. They have 20 seconds to accept; the current host stays in control until acceptance. This transfers the game-host role, not VRChat’s instance ownership or master role.

What if the host goes AFK?

After five minutes without meaningful activity, another player can Claim Host. A visible/audible 20-second warning gives the host time to use Keep Hosting or resume meaningful input. If the host leaves, claiming becomes available immediately. Only one claim can be pending.

The creator has tested Pass Host and AFK Claim Host in uploaded play. Less common cases such as claimant departure and simulation-owner suspension still need expanded testing.

Going AFK

This is different from the host claim above. Stay ready, open Host and tap AFK next round: you still ride out with the crew, but the next round's difficulty, quotas and supply count are worked out as if you were watching from dispatch, and the roster shows [AFK] next to your name. Stepping outside the van area during a round clears it on its own, as does tapping the button again. If you spend an entire round inside the van area without ever stepping out, the game marks you AFK for the next round by itself and tells you on the result screen. Someone who was AFK when a round began and then comes back keeps every cash and XP reward from that round but earns no commendation for it.

What else can the host do?

Start ready players, Restart contract, or Cancel round / return to dispatch. Start is a click and begins the countdown; restart and cancellation retain confirmation. Restart keeps this contract’s paid claims. Cancelling preserves earnings and ends the round; the next start is a fresh contract. Ready choices persist after completion or cancellation. The host can remain unready and spectate.

Why did the round start without me?

Only players ready at departure join the contract. New arrivals start unready. Ready / Unready stays on until you opt out, and changes during a round apply to the next departure. See the joining rules.

Why can’t I see the live field map?

The dispatch map is for spectators at dispatch. Joining the crew, being downed, or waiting to enter as a participant removes the dispatch view. Crew in the field have their own map on the van's monitor: it plots the crew with their names, and the spook only during extraction or while it stands in the open near the van after someone has seen it. A missing or delayed network snapshot displays a waiting/tracking-age message.

Can I watch a specific player?

Yes, from dispatch on PC. Every crew dot on the dispatch map carries a name; tap the dot, the name, or the roster in the legend column, and the screen on the office window shows an over-the-shoulder view of that player. The spook can be picked too. Tap again, tap CLOSE VIEW, or walk away from dispatch to close it. Crew in the field can do the same from the van: tap a teammate on the van's monitor and the old security CRT on the driver-side wall shows them. The spook can be watched there only while the van map shows it: once you have sighted it yourself, and then only during extraction or while it stands in the open near the van. It never appears in that view before you have seen it yourself. Walking away from the van closes the view. The view is PC-only for now (the mobile builds show the names and roster but refuse the camera), and whether the watched avatar actually appears depends on your own avatar visibility settings and distance culling.

Why does Tab stop me on desktop?

That happened in the tested VRChat client, including another world. Use the direct keyboard shortcuts during movement. I can pin the menu for reading, but Tab is still needed for clicking.

What makes a useful bug report?

Tell the playtest organizer the platform, approximate time, number of players, area, spook, and what happened before the issue. Include whether you were host or spectator, a screenshot if useful, and the build being tested. Avoid posting raw client logs publicly; they can contain account or session details.
