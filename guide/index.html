Your first shift

Development build: art, balance, and features are still changing. Spectator radar and AI diagnostics are temporary development features, not promises for the finished game.

1. Get ready at dispatch

Wait for your saved progress to finish loading. Choose Ready / Join to take part, or Watch / Withdraw to spectate. When everyone enrolled is ready, a five-second countdown starts. Becoming unready cancels an automatic countdown.

The game host can Force Start with at least one ready player. Only players ready at departure join the initial crew. A solo player uses the same ready-up flow.

Find Controls & Accessibility beside the ready board. Try the practice station before spending supplies; practice does not spend session purchases or award progression.

2. Read the contract

A trip has three stops. Each begins with a 20-second briefing for the contract, shop, and loadout. The two areas alternate and each stop uses a different killer. These are current tuning values, not fixed release promises.

3. Head into the field

The expedition lasts up to 150 seconds. Carry orange cargo cases back to the van and repair green equipment. Repairs retain progress when interrupted. Every required objective can be done alone without a purchased item or specific perk.

Late joins never raise an active stop’s quota. Departures or withdrawals can lower requirements while preserving finished work. Downed players and teammates sheltering at the van still count.

4. Regroup and extract

The covered campfire refuge beside the van is protected throughout the stop. Enter and leave freely. The current van body is a solid placeholder; use the covered exterior, not the van’s body as an entrance.

Finishing the quota opens 30 seconds of extraction early. Running out of expedition time triggers emergency extraction instead. Get back before departure: it does not wait indefinitely for missing players. Transit takes 10 seconds and uses a fade/teleport instead of driving standing avatars around.

Two completed contracts count as a successful shift; all three give the best report. Staying safe without participating does not earn activity rewards.

If you go down

You normally start with two health. A short immunity interval prevents immediate repeated hits. A downed player has a 12-second rescue window; a teammate can perform a three-second rescue, or two seconds with Field Medic.

If no able teammate remains, including solo play, the rescue wait is skipped. Otherwise, when the rescue window expires, an eight-second recovery returns you to the van. Dropped cargo can be recovered. Capture never removes banked XP or survival points. Second Wind allows a six-second field recovery on the first down of a stop.

Joining a trip already in progress

Use Ready / Join from dispatch. During an expedition you can opt in at the van. Arrivals during extraction or transit enter at the next briefing. Joining does not generate extra field supplies or increase the current quota.
