Your first shift

Development build: art, balance, and features are still changing. Spectator radar and AI diagnostics are temporary development features, not promises for the finished game.

1. Get ready at dispatch

Wait for your saved progress to finish loading. Choose Ready / Join to take part, or Watch / Withdraw to spectate. When everyone enrolled is ready, a five-second countdown starts. Becoming unready cancels an automatic countdown.

The game host can Force Start with at least one ready player. Only players ready at departure join the initial crew. A solo player uses the same ready-up flow.

Find Controls & Accessibility beside the ready board. Try the practice station before spending supplies; practice does not spend session purchases or award progression.

2. Read the contract

A trip has three stops. Each begins with a 20-second briefing for the contract, shop, and loadout. The two areas alternate and each stop uses a different killer. These are current tuning values, not fixed release promises.

The crew waits at the van for the first 10 seconds of the briefing, so there is time to shop and plan together. A glowing line marks the edge of the waiting area, and Head out appears in your status when the last 10 seconds begin and you are free to leave. Shopping and selling at the van work the whole time.

3. Head into the field

The expedition lasts up to 150 seconds. Carry orange cargo cases back to the van and repair green equipment. Repairs retain progress when interrupted. Every required objective can be done alone without a purchased item or specific perk.

Generators start dry. One fuel tank is hidden in the field each stop: carry it to a generator and touch it against the machine to prime that one, then take the tank on to the next. A primed generator takes 10 seconds of unbroken work to repair, or 7.5 with the Mechanic perk.

Late joins never raise an active stop’s quota. Departures or withdrawals can lower requirements while preserving finished work. Downed players and teammates sheltering at the van still count.

The field is not the same twice. Every stop rolls fresh obstacles — hay bales, broken-down vehicles, fallen trees, crates and drums — alongside the usual random supplies, and worksite front doors can be opened and closed while you work. See know the threats for how the hunter deals with a door you shut behind you.

4. Regroup and extract

The covered campfire refuge beside the van is protected throughout the stop. Enter and leave freely. The current van body is a solid placeholder; use the covered exterior, not the van’s body as an entrance.

Finishing the quota opens 30 seconds of extraction early. Running out of expedition time triggers emergency extraction instead. Get back before departure: it does not wait indefinitely for missing players. Transit takes 10 seconds: the view fades to black over one second and stays dark with a short next-stop message while walking pauses, then fades in at the next van. Standing avatars are never driven around. Menus cannot be opened during transit. The crew arrives spread around the van on separate spots rather than stacked on one point, and the same applies whenever the game moves you — a restart, a recovery, or returning to dispatch.

Two completed contracts count as a successful shift; all three give the best report. Staying safe without participating does not earn activity rewards.

If you go down

You normally start with two health. A short immunity interval prevents immediate repeated hits. Going down puts you on the floor: you crawl slowly, you cannot jump, and you drop whatever you were carrying. Your first down of a stop gives you 20 seconds; every later down in the same stop gives you 10.

Getting someone up is a hold, like a repair. One rescuer takes 10 seconds, and every extra pair of hands takes a quarter off that — 7.5 seconds for two, 5.6 for three. Field Medic halves whatever the crew's time is. While anyone is holding the revive your countdown stops; if they let go, half of the time they put in is added back to your clock, so a partial revive still buys you something.

If no able teammate remains, including solo play, your countdown is cut to a few seconds rather than run in full — nothing can answer it, but you still get that long on the floor. Otherwise, when your countdown expires, what happens depends on when you went down. In the first half of a stop an eight-second recovery returns you to the van. After halfway you are out for the rest of that stop: you return to dispatch, can watch the live field map, and rejoin the crew automatically when the next stop starts. Dropped cargo can be recovered either way. Capture never removes banked XP or survival points. Second Wind still allows a six-second field recovery on the first down of a stop.

Joining a trip already in progress

Use Ready / Join from dispatch. During an expedition you can opt in at the van. Arrivals during extraction or transit enter at the next briefing. Joining does not generate extra field supplies or increase the current quota.
