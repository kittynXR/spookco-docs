Controls & accessibility

Use the Controls & Accessibility panel beside dispatch’s ready board. You can also reach it from the van and personal inventory. Preferences are saved separately for desktop, VR, and touch.

Choose your language

On a first visit, the game waits for your saved profile, then shows a bilingual English/Japanese picker if no supported language is saved. On desktop, press 1 for English or 2 for Japanese in that initial picker, or hold Tab and click. In VR, use the personal-menu pointer on the floating picker.

Change it later under Controls → i18n / Language. The choice is saved across visits and shared between your desktop and VR profiles; resetting controls does not reset language. It changes only your own view, so the crew can use different languages. Website language links do not change your in-world setting. Branding, legal notices, and developer diagnostics retain their original text.

On the tested desktop client, Tab stops movement. This also happened in another world. Plan cursor interaction while stationary, preferably in the refuge. Shift+Tab may not open the menu while sprinting; release sprint first. I keeps a reading panel visible but does not unlock the mouse: hold Tab to click.

The six numbered shortcuts work without Tab, including with Shift held. They can be rebound, and conflicting bindings are reported. Reserve selection swaps the chosen reserve into the equipped slot; it does not draw or consume it. Press 2 to draw, then make a fresh Use input. A saved Tab Hold / Stay open preference is available.

VR: wrist or floating panel

Flick the right stick up or down to put the next item in your hand: hands free, your gear, then each consumable you carry. Pull the trigger on that hand to use it. Nothing needs confirming and no menu needs opening. At dispatch, hold the stick up for about half a second to ready up or unready, and hold it down to open the menu; the ready post on the floor by the ramp does the same by touch. Choose either wrist and either equipment hand. Holding the stick down is how the menu opens; there is no button on the wrist, and opening the menu does not freeze movement.

Wrist mode keeps the panel attached to your chosen wrist, so it travels with your arm. Floating mode places the panel once, level in front of you, at the moment you open it; switching tabs never moves it again. Height, distance and size are adjustable for seated play and different avatar scales.

The floating panel closes itself when you move more than three metres from where it was placed, when the expedition starts, when you pick up cargo, and when you take a hit or go down. If it ends up out of reach, the launcher — which sits below your wrist while the panel is open, clear of the Close corner — closes it, and pressing again places a fresh panel in front of you. Recenter in settings does the same. Menus cannot be opened during transit between stops.

Buttons that duplicate VRChat's own controls are hidden unless the current situation needs them, so the panel stays compact. Your wrist carries one thing: a readout card that shows whenever you look toward that hand within arm's reach: the round, your cash, health and level, anything acting on you (soda, Adrenaline, slowed, fog, EMF bearing) and what is in your hand. Alerts stay visible whether or not the panel is open. Wrist HUD +/- on the Controls tab (office board or menu) sets the card's size, saved with your profile.

Personal items follow the chosen hand. Cargo uses normal pickup controls, or the accessible Carry / Drop actions. If a native pickup occupies the equipment hand, stow or move it deliberately; personal equipment must not forcibly release it.

Phone and tablet

Use the screen’s MENU, USE ITEM, and COLLECT / WORK buttons. The world-space lobby boards are also present, but the screen controls are the intended touch shortcut. Inside inventory, select equipment, consumables, or reserves; use the separate Use action to activate the selected item.

The iPhone build has been tried by the creator. Final touch layout and ergonomics still need broader device testing.

Preferences and practice

  • Hold or toggle repair/rescue interactions.
  • Accessible cargo carrying with the same reservation, penalty, delivery, and drop rules.
  • Wrist/equipment handedness, floating panel placement/size, and the wrist HUD size.
  • Desktop key rebinding with conflict feedback and reset defaults.
  • Separate persisted control profiles for each input family.
  • Stick item select, on by default in VR: neutral deadzone 0.25, activation threshold 0.70, dwell 150 ms. Return to neutral before another step; each step equips outright.

These settings control SpookCo actions, not VRChat’s native movement bindings. The free practice bay is closed during live playtesting; its console and supplies are hidden and answer with a notice if reached. Selecting or collecting an item never activates it automatically. The lobby's two big boards stand in the dispatch office, front right of spawn.
