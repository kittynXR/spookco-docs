Equipment & field supplies

Your inventory

You have one reusable equipment slot, one equipped consumable, two reserve consumables, and one separate task-cargo slot. Duplicates are allowed; each occupies a slot. Purchases and scavenged supplies use the same three-slot pouch.

Only one personal item is actively held at a time. Switching personal items does not discard cargo. Selecting a reserve swaps it with the equipped consumable. Collecting, selecting, and drawing do not automatically use an item; consuming one does not activate the next.

On desktop, carried cargo and fuel use small HUD indicators, and the selected personal item appears as an icon with its name. These replace the obstructing first-person held models. Dropping cargo, docking fuel, using a consumable, or stowing an item clears the corresponding indicator. VR keeps normal hand models, and carried objectives remain visible to other players.

Reusable equipment

Unlocks require both a banked XP level and a lifetime commendation milestone. Commendations are not spent. Later equipment is deliberately a longer-term goal.

Equipment Level Commendations Role
Wind-up decoy 1 0 Place a distraction to pull the hunter’s investigation away.
Instant camera 4 12 A close-range flash can stun the killer; cooldowns and post-stun immunity limit repeated use.
EMF pager 12 120 Passive proximity information about the killer.
Trip alarm 18 400 Place one temporary alarm. A nearby killer triggers a positional signal and a brief stun; committed charges continue through it.
Track shoes 24 1,200 Passive 10% movement bonus, within the shared bonus cap.

Pager and shoes work without an active Use effect. Gear selection happens during shopping/loadout windows. Active gear has cooldowns; reconnecting or resetting must not refresh them.

Cash consumables

These prices and effects describe the current baseline and may be rebalanced.

Supply Cash Effect
Field ration 18 Restores one health when below your maximum.
Sprint soda 16 Eight-second movement boost; total positive movement bonuses cap at 20%.
Rescue flare 22 Rescues a nearby downed teammate within 12 metres. Needs a valid teammate.
Repair tape 20 Fixes one incomplete generator task step. You still press Start Engine yourself.
Smoke canister 24 Ten seconds of visual concealment. Blocks killer sight through its cloud, but not hearing or attacks already in motion.
Scrap bolt bundle 12 Toss a distraction onto nearby ground. Shares the decoy's resistance window; committed attacks ignore it.
Door brace 18 Brace a nearby closed door for up to 45 seconds. Adds four seconds to one normal breach; charges break through. Opening the door removes it.
Tool oil 20 Use beside an unfinished fueled generator for 25 seconds of easier startup controls and half the work-noise range.
Flash bulb 24 One aimed camera-strength flash. Shares stun resistance with the camera and trip alarm.
Emergency battery 28 Recharges your equipped active gear. Requires an active cooldown; an already armed trip alarm prevents recharging that alarm.

The shop has three supply pages. Perks and consumables can complement each other: oil provides the same control assistance as Mechanic and combines with Silent Tools for quieter work, while a flash bulb provides a one-use alternative when your reusable slot holds something else. Smoke does not make you bulletproof or silence your movement.

If the effect has no valid target, the request is rejected without spending the item. The shop guarantees a chosen supply, while field scavenging trades time and risk for a shared find.

A repaired generator recharges active gear twice as fast within six metres, during work and extraction. Solid walls block its power, and multiple generators do not stack. A normal 30-second cooldown takes about 15 seconds if you stay connected; Quickdraw reduces that to about 12. The HUD and wrist show GENERATOR: GEAR RECHARGE 2x while connected. EMF and track shoes are passive, so they do not recharge. Power does not protect you from killers or extend extraction.

Selling

Use Sell equipped consumable on the Shop page to sell the supply in your equipped slot. Selling is available in the same windows as buying, not in the field. Select it again within four seconds (or hold the button) to confirm. Bought supplies refund half their price; supplies found in the field refund a quarter and are marked (found) in your inventory. The practice bay is closed in this build, so its discard action is unavailable.

Shared field loot

Each stop rolls supplies at distinct authored locations. They are global pickups: once one player successfully collects an item, nobody else can collect that spawn. A full pouch shows Pouch Full and leaves it available.

Starting participants Shared items
1 1–2
2 1–3
4 2–4
8 3–7
12 4–9
16 6–12

All ten cash consumables can appear, with duplicates allowed. Smoke, braces and oil are slightly less common than the original supplies; bulbs and batteries are rarer. Solo stop rolls omit the teammate-focused rescue flare. Supplies are optional: a contract never requires finding one.

A pickup goes into the equipped consumable slot if empty, otherwise the first empty reserve. It is stored without being drawn. Collecting supplies awards no XP or cash. Unclaimed supplies disappear when the stop ends; collected supplies stay in your session pouch. Joins, host changes, restarts, and returning to dispatch do not reroll or replenish previously claimed supplies.
