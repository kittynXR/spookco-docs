Killer baseline

The creator accepts this as a useful working foundation. Fine tuning is deliberately paused while game and environment design improve. Do not mistake that decision for final AI or difficulty acceptance.

Movement sensing uses horizontal player speed: greater than 0.6 m/s starts walking noise, greater than 3 m/s uses running range. Detection distance is spherical. Soft Soles multiplies movement ranges by 0.75. Safe, downed/ineligible, and suspended players are excluded. This does not inspect microphone input.

Visible and audible candidates are distinct. Vision takes priority; sound behind cover starts investigation without granting sight-only warnings/attacks. New sound evidence refreshes at most every 0.8 seconds. When a player becomes quiet or leaves hearing range, the last evidence and deadline stop following them.

Rattletrap remains blind, with the largest movement ranges and separate task-noise investigation. Usher investigates after a broken warning and must warn afresh when it visually reacquires.

Turning corners and searching

Observed movement can project a short continuation, bounded to five metres. Mode 6 searches alternate nearby reachable NavMesh probes with authored route anchors. Missing route anchors no longer cause instant abandonment. Searches have both time and point budgets, so the configured duration is a ceiling rather than a guaranteed wait.

Search pauses sweep left/right for 2.2 seconds through a ±100° range. Moving patrol/search adds bounded lateral scanning (±28° patrol, ±45° search) without stopping navigation. Actual facing drives vision and presentation. Synced checkpoint fields preserve probe position, progress, reference heading, and sensing state.

The hearing pass did not increase speeds. The baseline uses roughly 3.25 m/s pursuit, 3.1 for Rattletrap and 3.65 for a Hitchhiker burst; player walk/run are 2.7/4.3 before modifiers. There is no claim that these values produce fair pressure for every party size.

Presentation and debug surfaces

The world killer model and spectator map use the same always-active feed. Non-owners wait for a matching pose, snap on the first valid sample/round change, then interpolate nearby updates. Feed data includes warning/hazard/decoy state and hearing ranges.

The map’s gold vision outline is clipped against cover; blue rings show walking/running hearing. Explicit sorting keeps them above terrain. Only eligible dispatch spectators can view tracking. The map is a horizontal projection, so vertical cover and actual head visibility can differ.

Host → AI diagnostics is an optional independent reader for owner IDs, heartbeat age, path state, feed receipt, presentation readiness, and movement information. Radar visibility and diagnostics are development features; decide whether to remove or restrict them before release.

Deferred work and acceptance

  • Fair search persistence and pressure distribution for solo through 16 participants.
  • Human evasion across multiple corners, noisy work, decoys, and changing routes.
  • Roadkill charge visibility versus actual hits under latency.
  • Hitchhiker warp presentation and safe relocation tells.
  • Switchboard hazard cover rules, warning/active distinction, and alternate escape routes.
  • Unreachable targets with reachable alternatives and owner changes in every mode.
  • Cooldown/stun immunity and strong-perk builds without unavoidable chains.

The local lanes cover navigation, frozen evidence, hearing boundaries/perks, search expiry, synced metadata, and rendered rings/cones. The fresh uploaded fairness and migration matrix remains open.
