Systems & architecture

The game-host role and simulation ownership are independent. Passing Host changes who can operate the lobby/round controls; the simulation owner keeps advancing gameplay without waiting for host input.

Authority and transactions

Network entry points validate the actual caller, eligibility, and relevant revisions. A local click never grants an item. The ledger owner commits pouch changes and the shared claim in one synchronized object, with claimant/grant identity and duplicate protection. Full capacity rejects collection without claiming the pickup.

Loot is rolled once per trip/stop. Restart Current Stop reuses the area, killer, manifest, and claims, but starts a clean objective attempt. Return to Dispatch keeps the trip open and records resumeStop, so the next departure resumes at that stop (or the next one if the stop was finished). Only the stop 3 report and the host's End Trip (phases 0–1) close a trip; the next departure after that is a fresh trip with new rolls. Every stop start bumps attemptSerial and clears that stop's field progress, while the once-per-trip objective and result reward masks prevent repeated payouts. Once-per-stop abilities and equipment cooldowns are also preserved.

Transit between stops no longer teleports to the old van. Crew stay behind a black hold with locomotion paused, leased every session tick while phase 5 lasts plus a 5-second grace and a 20-second cap. The stop-start teleport runs under black and fades in over one second. Opening menus is refused during the hold.

Selling the equipped consumable is a ledger transaction allowed in the same windows as buying. Bought supplies refund 50% and scavenged (found) supplies 25%; legacy pouches use bits 9–11 for found flags. New pouches set marker bit 15, use four bits per item, and store found flags in bits 12–14. Reads support both versions; a mutation migrates all three slots and provenance together. Empty remains zero. Sell descriptors use bit 4 for found provenance, separate from the item ID.

The layout authority follows the simulation owner. Each stop attempt rolls one variant (or nothing) per authored site and an open/closed state per door, then verifies that every authored destination in the area is still reachable once the obstacles have carved navigation; it re-rolls twice before falling back to a clear field. Door toggles are owner-validated requests — able crew, within reach, in a running stop, not broken, past a cooldown, and with nobody in the doorway or the leaf's swing. An attempt first seen mid-stop adopts a clear field rather than dropping obstacles around players.

Initial quotas and loot counts use the participating-player snapshot. Joins do not increase active quotas or supplies. Departures, withdrawal, and sustained suspension may lower outstanding requirements without erasing finished work. If nobody remains active, return to the lobby without completion rewards.

Temporary equipment effects

The equipment authority follows the session owner and checkpoints 32 shared effect slots, source player IDs, absolute expiry times and the attempt serial. Oil also records the owner identity per crew slot. A new attempt invalidates old effects, and reused crew slots cannot inherit oil. Receivers reconstruct local pooled visuals and positional alarm audio from synchronized state; no new networked objects are spawned per use.

Smoke intersects both head and torso visibility segments, including close contact, without suppressing hearing or physical attack resolution. Braces add one four-second normal door-breach delay; crew opening and charge destruction clear them. Flash bulbs and alarms share the camera stun immunity; bolts share decoy resistance. Battery use requires an active gear cooldown and cannot stack armed alarms. Invalid effects return false before the ledger spends an item. Useful interruption rewards reuse the existing bounded support grant.

Briefing countdown and spawn placement

The 15-second briefing (shortened from 20 s on 2026-09-29) is free: the van hold that kept each client's own crew member inside the refuge for the first ten seconds was retired on 2026-09-24 (see Docs/BriefingHoldRemoval-2026-09-24.md). No walls, marker, pull-back or lease remain; crew may leave at any second.

_BriefingCountdown() shows the countdown only while the phase is briefing, the local slot is alive crew and the synced deadline is plausible (0 < remaining <= briefingSeconds + 2); a stale or absurd deadline shows nothing. The HUD and status read "BRIEFING — shop at the van or head out (Ns)" and the wrist "BRIEFING Ns". Shopping and selling are gated by _Shopping (briefing inside the refuge, or dispatch); pickups are open through the briefing while delivery, priming and generator work wait for the expedition. The field vans' Shop + Inventory and Perk selection boards show only during the briefing and go blank at the phase flip; the personal menu keeps the inventory actions in the field.

User layer 22 stays reserved as SpookWorldBuilder.GuardLayer (players collide, the descriptor's Interact Passthrough bit 0 is set) for invisible walkway guards; world visuals live on DecorLayer (0) and only eye-locked veils, banners and prone seats on OverlayLayer (2).

Game-driven moves no longer stack the crew on one point. Sixteen authored, ground-seated spots sit under each van anchor and sixteen at dispatch, 1.5 m apart, clear of solid geometry, inside the refuge's .85 m spawn inset and facing the anchor's direction. They are used by transit arrival, stop start, restart, resume, late join, manual respawn at the refuge, recovery return, Watch, return to dispatch and the report. The local spot index is the player's rank by ledger slot — among crew for a van, among registered players for dispatch — where rank 0 keeps the original anchor, so solo positions are unchanged, and higher ranks map into spots 1–15 without ever wrapping back onto the anchor. Missing spot data falls back to the anchor, and VRChat's own join spawn still uses the single descriptor spawn. Two overlaps are accepted: more than 16 eligible players re-using spots 1–15 and a late joiner landing on a spot a teammate still occupies. A spectator who respawns during a trip stays on the world spawn at dispatch and is never moved to the van. A first-half recovery lands instead at the nearest recovery stone inside the stop's tier when one is nearer than the van (Docs/RecoveryStones-2026-09-24.md); the stones are shown only in phases 2–3 of the current area, inside the tier and before halfway (_LateInStop), and _RecoveryPost returns -1 after halfway so a vanished stone never receives anyone.

Persistence

Wait for OnPlayerRestored before writes, purchases, and grants. The permanent profile holds XP, commendations, perk selection, reusable gear, and player identity token. Separate VR/desktop/touch keys hold controls.

A synchronized random live-session key survives trips, resets, role changes, and ordinary ownership migration. A bounded 128-record history stores session cash, pouch, cooldowns, inventory revisions, grant identity, and reward masks. The current wallet is updated during play; the history index changes only on session entry/eviction. Old prototype wallet data has a migration path.

The implemented goal is same-session rejoin and A → B → A restoration without duplicated loot or refunded consumption. Actual cloud timing, interrupted writes, and concurrent-client race behavior remain uploaded acceptance work. No departure callback can guarantee a final cloud write after an abrupt crash.

Stable content IDs

  • Areas: 0 Camp Deadwood, 1 Last Chance Service Station, 2 Calder Nuclear Laboratory (draft, not in normal trips).
  • Killers: 0 Groundskeeper, 1 Rattletrap, 2 Roadkill, 3 Hitchhiker, 4 Usher, 5 Switchboard.
  • Consumables: 0 empty, 1 ration, 2 soda, 3 flare, 4 tape, 5 smoke, 6 bolts, 7 brace, 8 oil, 9 flash bulb, 10 battery.
  • Reusable gear: 0 decoy, 1 camera, 2 EMF pager, 3 track shoes, 4 trip alarm. Display order follows unlock thresholds without changing stable IDs.
  • Perk bits 0–18: Spotter, Courier, Mechanic, Field Medic, Scrapper, Soft Soles, Adrenaline, Quickdraw, Hardhat, Second Wind, Silent Tools, Ear Defenders, Escape Artist, Braced, Clear Lenses, Sixth Sense, Trailbreaker, Inspector, Focused Beam (level 15, added 2026-09-23). The perk pages size themselves from the catalogue, nine rows each, and the career mastery pages follow them. The ten legacy indices and costs are unchanged. _SanitizePerks validates levels and budget while retaining affordable selections.

Preserve IDs when replacing art or changing display names. Three consumable slots allow duplicates. Old three-bit IDs migrate to the marked four-bit format on mutation. XP to the next level is 150 + 35 × (current level − 1) through level 40; 31,785 total XP. Final reward pacing has not been measured.

Capacity and transport limits

Runtime admission is still 16 despite several storage/feed arrays having 32 slots. The capacity increase is deferred. Larger storage arrays do not raise the admission cap.

The AI checkpoint and presentation stream are separate. The always-active manual feed requests updates at 4 Hz; matching trip/stop/area/revision is required. A new owner can recover from a fresher presentation pose under a recovery interval. Local decoding/interpolation tests do not prove Photon delivery or suspension recovery.

Generator power

Completed repair bits enable a six-metre, solid-geometry-occluded connection during expedition and extraction. The shared ledger owner samples able players with active gear every 0.25 seconds and subtracts one extra second per elapsed second from their existing absolute cooldown deadline. Two consecutive eligible samples bind to the same player and cooldown; attempts, phases and owner changes discard elapsed history. A sample contributes at most 0.5 seconds. Multiple generators do not stack.

Cooldown-only changes use the existing manual ledger snapshot without increasing inventory transaction revisions. The profile writer compares the saved cooldown as well as the revision so those snapshots persist. Power changes no rewards, health, contract quota, deadline or armed-alarm placement limits.

Optional salvage

The cargo pool has nineteen independent slots: required cases 0–15 and bonus cases 16–18. SpookObjectives checkpoints three bonus counts and nine area-local layout indices. On each stop it selects one, two or three distinct eligible anchors at least ten metres from the van for initial crews of 1–2, 3–8 or 9–16. Same-layout retries retain those choices.

Bonus pickup opens only during completed-contract extraction and closes at the existing deadline. Delivery maps the three bonus slots to ledger objective bits 26–28; required cargo, repair and fuel bits remain unchanged. The once-per-trip paid mask also hides already-paid bonuses after an attempt restart. Delivery pays twice crew-scaled cargo XP and $24, with existing identity-bound handoff credit and Scrapper rounding. Cargo pickup and delivery require aligned objectives, session and ledger authority.

The first eight seconds of completed extraction defer the all-aboard check while unpaid bonus work is available. This does not move the deadline, and everybody can still choose an early departure. Gold straps, case stencils, the extraction banner and HUD/wrist counts explain the option. Physical pickups and hands-free actions use the existing cargo routing.

Contribution accounting

Movement no longer calls participation. Cargo and repair use identity-bound shared contribution records; cargo needs a three-metre carry and repair helpers need 10% progress. Fuel priming has separate paid bits 21–25. The ledger's once-per-trip objective claim bounds finisher and helper awards together, including retries. Bounded rescue and real gear interruption remain independent paid categories.

A completed, contributed contract awards one commendation, and extracting alive from it adds one. Failed extraction does not pay a survival bonus. Eliminated contributors can receive contract credit. Final-shift XP/cash scales with the player's earned contract bits. The permanent sp key and balance are preserved; only the player-facing name changes. Current-attempt report arrays synchronize work/contract/extraction/shift XP, cash and commendations. Compact wallet fields ra, ro and rs preserve that report on a same-attempt reconnect.

Career mastery

The ledger synchronizes 13 counters per identity in mastery[416]: hauling, engineering, support and the ten killers. careerTitle[32] selects none (0) or track + 1. Job thresholds are 5/25/100; killer thresholds are 3/10/25. Titles are cosmetic roster text, with owner-validated selection through ledger action 8 and UI action 44. The existing perk navigator retains core/specialist pages and adds two career pages on every panel.

Credits are inside the same paid objective/support/extraction transaction and revision as their existing rewards. Objective helpers receive one mastery credit; support retains separate once-per-stop rescue and useful-gear bits. Killer credit requires the contributed extraction award. Paid masks survive stop retries. A newly ended trip retains the existing fresh-trip reward policy; this change does not impose a new replay restriction.

The permanent sc.profile.v1 dictionary adds a 13-number mastery list and title selection. Older killer masks migrate one proven escape per bit, without invented job history. Counts clamp to 0..1,000,000; malformed entries become zero. Reused slots replace all counters, while a returning cached identity merges each counter by maximum, matching existing permanent XP restoration. Simultaneous independent instances are not an additive distributed counter; uploaded concurrent-instance acceptance remains necessary. Progress is saved only after the owner snapshot is sent, alongside the existing wallet revision.
