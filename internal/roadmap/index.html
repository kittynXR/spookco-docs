Development field notes · Planning

Roadmap & open decisions

The original two-week plan remains a delivery framework. The current implementation moved ahead of final content, but not all acceptance gates are closed. September 15 raised killer pressure, gave the map per-stop obstacles and interactive doors, and added the briefing van hold with spread spawn spots. What all of it needs now is play data, not another local pass.

Next: make the stops distinct

  • Review solo and group routes in both areas and the readability of safe refuge access.
  • Define the van/campfire visual identity and decide whether the van needs a walkable interior.
  • Improve objective feedback and meaningful scavenging decisions.
  • Watch whether the ten-second briefing hold actually produces shared planning or just reads as a cage, and whether the spread spawn spots land people somewhere useful.
  • Choose a cohesive palette, lighting mood, modular environment kit, and six killer silhouettes.
  • Measure short-loop fun and pacing with ordinary players. Killers now match a running player and reach +20% at eight or more crew, with cover, doors and re-rolled field obstacles as the counterplay; tune from that play data rather than from another local pass.

Then: finish integration and scale

  • Implement the accepted 18-person instance overflow policy while keeping gameplay tuned for 1–16 participants.
  • Complete actual concurrent loot/purchase/cargo tests and reconnect A → B → A testing.
  • Exercise ready countdown races, late entry, withdrawal, and quota reductions.
  • Extend host-control testing beyond the user-confirmed Pass Host/AFK claim path to departures, cancellation, and suspension.
  • Verify VR wrist/floating inventory, touch ergonomics, desktop direct shortcuts, and all accessibility alternatives.

Then: balance, content freeze, and profiling

  • Tune rewards toward a meaningful upgrade around the first half-hour and 20+ hours to full perk capacity; neither timing is yet measured.
  • Test all six killers with starter solo loadouts in both areas and with larger groups.
  • Review charge/warp/hazard fairness, repeated stuns, and strong perk combinations.
  • Complete art/audio, warning clarity, credits, dawn report polish, and the final status of spectator radar/AI diagnostics.
  • Run 30-minute 1/16-player performance sessions, report world and public-avatar costs separately, and fix measured bottlenecks.

Release targets

The working plan targets a September 27, 2026 release candidate with a buffer before the planned October 1, 2026, 11:59:59 p.m. Pacific submission deadline. These are planning dates. Recheck the official contest rules, content requirements, and submission instructions before release; this site is not an eligibility ruling.

Produce matching PC, Android, and iOS builds, review SDK warnings and credits, upload manually, and submit manually. Do not equate successful asset-bundle compilation with a released world or contest submission.

Decisions still open

Decision Why it matters
18-person overflow behavior Creator exceptions can exceed a 16-person instance cap; runtime admission still needs handling.
Final spectator information Full sensing radar may undermine mystery; current display is useful for testing.
Debug controls in release Determine whether to strip, gate, or retain diagnostics.
Van interior versus exterior refuge Changes art effort, collision, capacity, and navigation.
Solo/group pressure and economy Current numbers are initial tuning; measure fun and progression. The accepted chase values (player run speed solo, +20% at 8+, a 130% lunge inside 3 m, a 1.5 s pause after a landed hit) are unmeasured against human evasion.
End trip reward policy End trip deliberately ships unguarded for testing: play stop 1, return to dispatch, End trip, depart again, and that stop's objective, contract, extraction and rescue rewards pay again with freshly rolled supplies. The per-stop rate resembles a full run, so the gain is skipping stops 2–3 and the trip bonus rather than a rate exploit. Options: carry the ended trip's paid and claim masks forward for visited stops, put a cooldown on End trip, or accept it. Decide before release, and do not read progression pacing from sessions that used it.
Briefing hold strength It is a courtesy constraint with no authority: a station, playspace drift or a modified client bypasses it, and roomscale walking against the barrier is still untested in a headset. Decide whether that is enough or whether the briefing needs a real gate.
Final art/audio scope Keep two areas and six identities achievable without increasing mobile cost excessively.
