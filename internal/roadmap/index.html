Development field notes · Planning

Roadmap & open decisions

The September 20 gameplay and atmosphere expansion is implemented locally. Its remaining release work is uploaded multiplayer acceptance, physical input and presentation review, economy tuning and target-device profiling. Preserve the distinction between completed implementation and unmeasured human play.

Next: make the stops distinct

  • Review solo and group routes in all three destinations and the readability of safe refuge access.
  • Paint the revamped van's livery, rust and stencils (paint brief in Docs/VanRevamp-2026-09-24.md); the walkable interior shipped on 2026-09-24.
  • Measure whether completed-generator recharge, optional extraction salvage and mastery goals make work feel rewarding.
  • The ten-second briefing hold read as a cage in playtests and was retired on 2026-09-24; watch whether the free 15-second briefing (shortened on 2026-09-29) still produces shared planning, and whether the spread spawn spots land people somewhere useful.
  • Choose a cohesive palette, lighting mood, modular environment kit, and the ten killer silhouettes.
  • Measure short-loop fun and pacing with ordinary players. Movement pressure now varies by killer and crew size; cover, doors, quiet movement, stale trails and committed firing lanes provide different responses. Tune from real play data.

Then: finish integration and scale

  • Keep capacity at sixteen; the creator deferred expansion to twenty or twenty-four players.
  • Complete actual concurrent loot/purchase/cargo tests and reconnect A → B → A testing.
  • Exercise ready countdown races, late entry, withdrawal, and quota reductions.
  • Extend host-control testing beyond the user-confirmed Pass Host/AFK claim path to departures, cancellation, and suspension.
  • Verify VR wrist/floating inventory, touch ergonomics, desktop direct shortcuts, and all accessibility alternatives.

Then: balance, content freeze, and profiling

  • Measure the revised early perk-point gains and longer-term mastery pacing before adopting an hours-to-unlock target.
  • Test all ten killers with starter solo loadouts in all three destinations and with larger groups.
  • Review charge/warp/hazard fairness, repeated stuns, and strong perk combinations.
  • Complete art/audio, warning clarity, credits, dawn report polish, and the final status of spectator radar/AI diagnostics.
  • Run 30-minute 1/16-player performance sessions, report world and public-avatar costs separately, and fix measured bottlenecks.

Release targets

The working plan targets a September 27, 2026 release candidate with a buffer before the planned October 1, 2026, 11:59:59 p.m. Pacific submission deadline. These are planning dates. Recheck the official contest rules, content requirements, and submission instructions before release; this site is not an eligibility ruling.

Produce matching PC, Android, and iOS builds, review SDK warnings and credits, upload manually, and submit manually. Do not equate successful asset-bundle compilation with a released world or contest submission.

Decisions still open

Decision Why it matters
Future capacity expansion Deferred; any increase beyond sixteen needs explicit scope, admission rules and performance evidence.
Final spectator information Full sensing radar may undermine mystery; current display is useful for testing.
Debug controls in release Determine whether to strip, gate, or retain diagnostics.
Van interior versus exterior refuge Decided 2026-09-24: a walk-through interior (floor, roof, outer walls solid) inside the protected refuge; the covered exterior keeps the capacity.
Solo/group pressure and economy Current numbers are initial tuning; measure fun and progression. The current per-killer pursuit ratios, crew scaling, warned lethal attacks, quiet-movement threshold and reward pacing need measurement against human evasion and ordinary play.
End trip reward policy End trip deliberately ships unguarded for testing: play stop 1, return to dispatch, End trip, depart again, and that stop's objective, contract, extraction and rescue rewards pay again with freshly rolled supplies. The per-stop rate resembles a full run, so the gain is skipping stops 2–3 and the trip bonus rather than a rate exploit. Options: carry the ended trip's paid and claim masks forward for visited stops, put a cooldown on End trip, or accept it. Decide before release, and do not read progression pacing from sessions that used it.
Free briefing The van hold was retired on 2026-09-24 (Docs/BriefingHoldRemoval-2026-09-24.md). Crew can now roam and pre-carry cargo and fuel for all 15 s (20 s before 2026-09-29) while the killer is dormant, reach the killer's fixed spawn before it appears, and the layout's re-roll window overlaps free crew. The clock was shortened to 15 s on 2026-09-29 and the van boards now close with it; watch whether crews still shop at the van.
Final art/audio scope Keep three areas and ten identities achievable without increasing mobile cost excessively.
