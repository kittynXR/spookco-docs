Development notesWORKING BUILD / SEPT 2026

Development field notes · September 20, 2026

Current project status

Gameplay expansion implemented and locally checked; not a release candidate. The September 20 update adds contribution-based rewards, quieter and occluded hearing, partial-cover visibility, warned lethal encounters, eighteen perks, ten consumables, five reusable tools, ten killers, optional salvage, generator recharge and persistent career titles. Capacity remains sixteen.

Dispatch now has darker baked interior lighting, faulty fixtures, passable decorative furniture, fog, a corrected sign, a visible departure countdown, a five-second master-start hold and an illuminated lounge CRT with a fake projected glow. The creator accepted the darker lighting during development. Local editor and ClientSim results do not establish final physical-device or uploaded multiplayer acceptance.

These notes are unlisted, public documentation. They are not linked from the player navigation or public search index. Do not put credentials, private playtester details, or raw logs here. Development-only spectator radar and AI diagnostics may change or be removed.

How to read the status

Implemented means the code and scene contain the feature. Locally checked means editor/static/ClientSim evidence exists. User confirmed identifies a reported uploaded playtest. Open means unfinished work or missing acceptance. These labels are deliberately not interchangeable.

Baseline snapshot

Gameplay checkpoint: b7b741c — persistent career mastery and cosmetic crew titles, following the September 20 expansion milestones. Unity remains 2022.3.22f1, VRChat SDK 3.10.5, AI Navigation 1.1.5. The shared editor scene contains other ongoing art/guide work; local source milestones deliberately preserve those changes rather than stage them wholesale.

See testing and release gates for current lane dates, platform bundle evidence and limitations. Earlier September 15 totals are historical evidence, not a claim that every old lane ran again for this expansion.

Next acceptance work

  1. Play the three-map loop with real crews, including all ten killers, optional salvage decisions and newly earned loadouts.
  2. Check quiet creeping, partial peeks, warned gunfire and physical cover on desktop and in roomscale VR.
  3. Exercise actual cloud saves, simultaneous interactions, late joins and owner departures under latency.
  4. Profile PCVR, Quest and iPhone with one and sixteen players; distinguish world costs from public-avatar costs.
  5. Judge economy pacing and mastery goals from ordinary play, then tune from those observations.
  6. Record uploaded test build, devices, party size and result before release.
